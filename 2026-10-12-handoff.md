# Device handoff — started 2026-10-06, current as of 2026-10-12

Current state and next steps are in **Where things stand** below; the dated updates after **Access** are the history.

## Goal
Build a voice-first Android device with a privileged agent and cloud AI inference. No calls or SMS needed. The RAVOZ V1 is throwaway hardware; its touchscreen is already unreliable.

## What happened
- The RAVOZ V1 runs stock Android 12. Its bootloader was locked. After a `fastboot reboot`, it stalled on the RAVOZ logo; pressing Power brought up a “Can't load Android system” recovery screen. **Factory data reset** got it booting again. The health-library warning on that screen did not identify the cause.
- Enabled **OEM unlocking** and USB debugging again after the reset. Fastboot reported `unlock_ability = 16777216` (rather than the usual `1`). The unlock succeeded after confirming with **Volume Up** on the phone. `fastboot getvar unlocked` returned `yes`; Android reported `ro.boot.flash.locked=0`.
- Booted the **non-GMS Android 13 ARM64 GSI** (`TQ3A.230805.001.A3`) through **DSU Loader**. This is a temporary system; stock Android 12 remains available through the DSU notification's **Restart** action. No OS image was flashed.
- On the GSI, ADB, Wi-Fi/internet, screen sleep/wake, battery reporting and charging worked. Android detected the touchscreen, speaker, mic and two cameras. *(Mic and speaker were tested and work: see the 2026-10-10 afternoon update.)* DSU's temporary `/data` is about 1.9 GB.

## Device facts (read over ADB on the DSU, 2026-10-09)
- SoC **MediaTek MT6761** (Helio A22, `MT6761V/WBB`), 4× Cortex-A53; RAM **2.8 GB** (~1.7 GB available while idle); screen 720×1600 @ 320 dpi.
- Kernel 4.19.191. Vendor partition is Android 12, **VNDK 31**. `ro.build.ab_update=true` (A/B) and `ro.boot.dynamic_partitions=true` (super partition); this is a standard Treble layout.
- **Virtual A/B** (`ro.virtual_ab.enabled=true`), current slot `_a`, vendor not VNDK-lite. Vendor fingerprint `RAVOZ/RAVOZ_V1_3_64/RAVOZ_V1_3_64:12/SP1A.210812.016/1692877872:user/release-keys` (3 GB / 64 GB variant).
- Running GSI: `Android/aosp_arm64/generic_arm64:13/TQ3A.230805.001.A3/10454027:user/release-keys`. It is a **`user`** build, so `adb root`, `adb remount`, `tinycap` and `tinyplay` are unavailable. *(That was the DSU. The phone now runs TrebleDroid `userdebug`, flashed to `system_a`; see Phase 1.)*
- Bootloader unlocked (`ro.boot.flash.locked=0`). The DSU had been up for about 68 h without a reboot.
- Because the SoC is MediaTek, the stock restore tool is **SP Flash Tool** (needs a scatter file), and BROM/`mtkclient` recovery is likely available. Neither has been verified yet.
- Stock Android 12 fingerprint and `fastboot getvar all` output have **not** been recorded yet.

## Where things stand (2026-10-12)
**Working on the phone** (repo: https://github.com/code-akram/or1, `main`; details in `app/README.md`; UI spec in `docs/ui-ledger.md`):
- `com.or1.agent` is the HOME app and the only UI. The agent is Claude Haiku 5.5 (adaptive thinking, medium effort, fixed in `agent.json`) through the Anthropic Java SDK, with streamed replies and prompt caching at a 1-hour TTL. Each message carries the local time.
- 12 tools: screen, act, open, device, clock, calendar, contacts, notifications, apps, shell (root through phh-su; `content query` runs as the app), memory, routines.
- **UI: "the Ledger"** (M1–M6, done 2026-10-12, see the update below). Each request is an *act*: a request, a list of changes ("receipts") with **Undo**, and a **live card** when the answer is a thing (timer, alarm, setting, notifications, contact, routine, calendar list, email, media). A **Now strip** in the top bar holds live chips (running timer, next alarm, next event, "N new" notifications → triage sheet, a running routine). The idle home shows today's undoable changes, then a one-line greeting and suggestions. Drawer: New chat, **Today** (every change from chats and routines, with Undo), Routines, **What or1 knows** (memory notes, editable), Developer details and Speak replies switches. Tapping `or1` in the status bar always returns home; Back closes the topmost layer.
- **Voice works**: hold the round button to talk (release sends, slide up for hands-free, slide left cancels), the mic icon dictates into the field, `+` → Voice mode is a hands-free listen → act → speak loop (Now strip and the turn's live card on screen; talk over or1 to interrupt it; replies kept to a short sentence via the `[Voice]` marker). Opt-in **Listen for “Hey or1”** (drawer) listens on the idle home and opens voice mode; it is unverified with a real voice (see the 2026-10-12 day update). Speech in is Google's on-device recogniser (English US pack downloaded 2026-10-12); speech out is Google TTS. Both sit behind `SpeechInput`/`SpeechOutput` (`Voice.kt`), ready for ElevenLabs.
- **No direct app access:** the system UI is locked down (no nav bar, shade, quick settings, heads-up or lock-screen notifications; launcher3 disabled). Apps run on a hidden, secure work display; while or1 works, the act card shows a live strip of it with a ring where it taps and "Watch or take over" (opens the full-screen, usable viewer). Apps there are muted while a request runs.
- No approvals (the user's decision): the agent acts without asking. Trust comes from receipts with Undo (instant local undo for settings, alarms, timers, calendar, contacts, routines and notes; in-app changes undo by asking or1), an irreversible marker, and an evidence screenshot taken just before committing taps. Planted instructions are handled by the prompt's untrusted-content rule, checked by the injection eval tasks.
- Calendar card rows have **Move** (finished by or1 from a prefilled composer; new `calendar move`) and **Delete** (here, with Undo). Media the agent starts **keeps playing after the turn** (`MediaKeeper`), with a Now chip to pause; the work display parks 2 min after playback stops.
- Routines (time or notification triggered, optionally named) run in their own thread; finished runs appear in the chat stream as their own acts (dismissable), with a chime. Exact alarms are re-armed on boot; a run waits for the network (waking the screen if needed) and retries once.
- Calendar through Etar with a local "Phone" calendar. Google apps (MindTheGapps 13 in `system_a`, uncertified GSF ID, demo account): Gmail and YouTube, driven on the work display.

**Quality:** the on-device eval (`proto`, 30 tasks incl. three prompt-injection tests) passes **30/30** on the current build (2026-10-12 12:53 phone time, after the voice/calendar/media changes; $0.0003 per task). CI runs 168 JVM unit tests (app) and 12 pytest tests (proto) on every push.

Timer undo/tracking presses the Clock app's notification buttons by their English titles. The email card parses Gmail's screen and shows nothing when unsure. The alarm tool sets the next occurrence of a time, not a date. Older activity-log entries keep their old labels.
